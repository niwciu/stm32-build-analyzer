# IAR Arm adapter design

## Decision

The adapter parses the text MAP generated by IAR ILINK and does not execute the
`.out` ELF/DWARF image or require `ilinkarm`, `ielfdump`, or `ielftool`. Numeric
`place in [from ... to ...]` declarations supply region capacities, placement
rows supply runtime sections, and an optional entry list supplies symbol
addresses and sizes.

ILINK section rows do not identify the ROM load address of initialized RAM
data. Such builds therefore retain runtime RAM results but return a structured
partial-analysis warning instead of guessing ROM usage. Likewise, absolute
`place at` declarations without a capacity are omitted with a warning.

## Verified scope

- IAR ILINK 7.80.1.28 is covered by a real compact MAP fixture from Mbed OS,
  redistributed under Apache-2.0.
- IAR ILINK 9.50.1 is covered by a documented synthetic fixture and remains
  experimental pending a redistributable real fixture.
- Symbolic, non-materialized placement bounds are rejected because they cannot
  provide numeric capacity.
- ILINK 8 and materially different older or newer layouts remain unsupported
  until separate fixtures establish compatibility.
